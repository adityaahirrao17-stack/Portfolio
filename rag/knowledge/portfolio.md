## About Aditya
Aditya Ahirrao is a UI/UX designer and an alumnus of the Services Preparatory Institute (SPI), now a Computer Science and Design undergraduate specialising in AI systems, backend engineering and full-stack application development. He designs and builds AI products end to end, from the retrieval pipelines underneath to the interfaces people actually use.

## Design Society
The Design Society (DeSoc) is the design club of KKWIEER that organises tech and non-tech events. Aditya is its President and earlier served as Vice President. He also built the official DeSoc website showcasing the society's events, team and achievements.

## Young Indians
Young Indians (Yi) KKWIEER Chapter is a movement for Indian youth. As Chairperson of Branding, Aditya leads branding for social-cause and youth-empowerment drives. He also served as Co-Chair of Road Safety in 2025.

## INNOVERA, ISTE and TEDx
INNOVERA is a national-level hackathon, where Aditya led design and branding end to end. He is a Core Committee Member of ISTE (Indian Society for Technical Education) since 2025, and was a Design Team Member for TEDxKKWIEER, an international event themed "Rise Through Resonance".

## Talks
Aditya was an invited speaker at the ISTE Master Students Programme (MSP) 2025 with a talk titled "Perfection Paradox".

## Smart India Hackathon 2025
Aditya's team won Smart India Hackathon 2025 as a national winner for the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare problem statement, at BPUT Rourkela, Odisha, with Swasthyam: an AI-powered healthcare system and national campaign aimed at reducing edible oil consumption. He was later felicitated as a hackathon winner by the Department of Computer Science & Design, KKWIEER.

## Defence preparation
At the Services Preparatory Institute, a Government of Maharashtra military school preparing students for UPSC NDA, Aditya reached the SSB conference-out stage at 17 SSB Bangalore (NDA 150) on 2 April 2023.

## Other work
Medication Reminder: a smart medication reminder app with notifications and tracking. Smart Heal (Runverve): a fitness tracking application with AI-powered insights and recommendations. Dams of Nashik Ride: a long-distance cycling expedition of about 107 km across the prominent dams of Nashik.
